## 1. Business Overview

**NeoTechnology Solutions LLC** is a US-based technology services company that builds, integrates, and automates e-commerce infrastructure for online merchants in the GCC and the United States.

**One-sentence positioning:**  
*The only US-incorporated e-commerce technology partner with deep GCC payment, compliance, and Arabic localisation expertise — at transparent, fixed project prices.*

### Mission
Remove the technical complexity that holds online merchants back. One contract. One point of accountability.

### Vision
Become the default technical implementation partner for GCC merchants going to market and US merchants going cross-border — within 5 years.

### Core Values
1. **Technical excellence** — Every delivery is production-grade, tested, documented
2. **Regulatory compliance** — ZATCA, CCPA, GDPR-aware; never cut corners
3. **Transparent pricing** — Fixed packages, no surprise invoices

## 3. Market Strategy

### 3.1 Primary Market: Saudi Arabia + GCC

**Why GCC first:**
- Founder has native market knowledge, language, and regulatory familiarity
- Regional partner provides warm introductions and local trust
- GCC e-commerce growing 15–20% CAGR with structural tailwinds (Vision 2030)
- Mada + Arabic + ZATCA requirements create a defensible niche that global agencies cannot easily enter

**Top GCC platforms to partner with:**
1. Salla — ~400,000 merchants, has a Partner Directory → list NTS as certified partner
2. Zid — Growing merchant base, has API marketplace → list automation integrations
3. Tap Payments — Arab-region payment gateway → become certified integration partner
4. Moyasar — KSA-native gateway → same

### 3.2 Secondary Market: United States

**Why US second:**
- Wyoming entity creates natural US credibility
- Shopify + WooCommerce are universal
- n8n + automation services highly valued by US DTC brands
- English content produces SEO traffic that compounds

**Target US segments:**
- Shopify merchants $100K–$2M GMV needing technical upgrades
- WooCommerce merchants struggling with plugin conflicts and performance
- DTC brands moving from Zapier to n8n

### 3.3 Positioning Statement

For online merchants in the GCC and US who need trustworthy technical implementation, NeoTechnology Solutions LLC is the e-commerce technology partner that combines the legal credibility of a US incorporated company with the Arabic-language expertise and GCC payment knowledge that no generalist agency can match — at fixed, transparent prices.

## 4. Sales Process

### 4.1 Client Journey

```
Awareness → Interest → Evaluation → Purchase → Delivery → Retention
```

**Awareness channels:**
- LinkedIn content (Arabic + English)
- Salla/Zid partner directories
- Google search (Arabic: إعداد متجر إلكتروني; English: Salla setup service)
- Referrals from regional partner
- Quick-fix tickets → upsell path

**Interest / Qualification:**
- Free 30-min scoping call (Calendly or phone)
- Output: recommend package, confirm budget, confirm timeline

**Evaluation:**
- Proposal sent within 24 hours of scoping call
- Includes: scope, timeline, deliverables, 3 reference projects (or case studies)
- Validity: 14 days

**Purchase:**
- E-signed contract (MSA + SOW)
- 50% deposit via Stripe or Mercury transfer
- Project kickoff scheduled within 48hrs

**Delivery:**
- Staging environment → client review → revisions → go-live
- Final 50% balance collected on delivery
- 30-day support window activated

**Retention:**
- 30-day check-in email
- Upsell to managed n8n, additional automation flows
- Referral request (offer $100 credit for referred client who closes)

## 9. Exit / Long-Term Strategy

This is a **lifestyle + platform business** in Year 1–2. In Year 3+, two paths emerge:

**Path A — Grow to agency**: Hire team, systemise delivery, aim for $1M+ revenue. Valuation multiple for service businesses: 2–4× EBITDA = $500K–$1M at $250K EBITDA.

**Path B — Productise**: Convert the n8n + automation expertise into a SaaS product (e.g., "Automation-as-a-Service for GCC merchants"). Higher multiple (5–10× ARR). Requires separate product investment.

**Path C — Maintain lifestyle business**: Keep founder-led, $200K–$300K annual EBITDA, minimal overhead. Fully viable given the business model.

No decision needed in Year 1. Focus is execution.
