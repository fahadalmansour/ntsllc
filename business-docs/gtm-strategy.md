# NeoTechnology Solutions LLC — Go-to-Market Strategy
**Date:** May 2026  
**Horizon:** 12 months (May 2026 – April 2027)

---

## Objective

Close the first 10 paying clients within 90 days. Reach $15,000/month revenue by Month 6. Build a referral engine that produces 3+ inbound leads per month by Month 9.

---

## 1. The First Client Problem

Every services business faces the same chicken-and-egg problem: clients want proof of work, but you need clients to create proof of work. NTS solves this in three ways:

### 1A — Launch Offer (Weeks 1–4)
Offer the first 5 clients a **Starter package at $799** (vs. $999) in exchange for:
- Allowing NTS to publish a case study (anonymised if preferred)
- A written or video testimonial within 14 days of launch
- Permission to screenshot/record the store as a portfolio piece

This is a $200 discount per client = $1,000 total cost for 5 testimonials. Worth it.

### 1B — Regional Partner Pipeline (Weeks 1–8)
The Saudi regional partner (Fahad Saad Fahad Almansour Office for Electronic Services) has existing business relationships. Activate this pipeline immediately:
- Share the NTS service brochure (Arabic version — see below) with partner's network
- Offer 10% referral commission on any closed project
- Set up a WhatsApp Business profile for Saudi inquiries

### 1C — Quick-Fix as Acquisition Channel (Ongoing)
Quick-fix tickets ($99–$299) are low-commitment entry points. A client who trusts NTS to fix their SSL will naturally consider NTS for a full store rebuild. Run a simple Facebook/Instagram ad targeting Saudi/GCC store owners with "Is your site slow or broken? $99 fix, 24hrs."

## 2. Channel Strategy

### Priority 1 — Referral (Highest ROI, CAC ~$0–50)

**Actions:**
- Build a referral programme: $100 account credit for any client whose referral closes a project
- Ask every completed client for one referral within the 30-day support window
- Maintain a contact list of every client → WhatsApp check-in at 3 months

**Target**: 30% of Year 1 clients come from referral by Month 6

### Priority 2 — Salla / Zid Partner Directories (Passive inbound)

**Actions:**
1. Apply to Salla Partner Directory at partners.salla.com — list NTS as a certified technical partner
2. Apply to Zid Marketplace
3. Create a complete profile with: Arabic description, package prices, response time guarantee, contact button
4. Keep profile updated with completed project count

**Target**: 2–3 inbound leads/month from directories by Month 4

### Priority 3 — LinkedIn Organic (GCC B2B)

**Content calendar (3 posts/week):**

| Day | Content type | Language | Topic examples |
|-----|-------------|---------|----------------|
| Mon | Educational | Arabic | "5 أشياء يجب فعلها قبل إطلاق متجرك الإلكتروني" |
| Wed | Case study / result | English or bilingual | "We built a Saudi fashion store in 9 days — here's what we automated" |
| Fri | Industry insight | Arabic or English | ZATCA update, new Salla feature, n8n release |

**Engagement rules:**
- Reply to every comment within 24 hours
- DM every profile like/share with a brief, non-salesy message
- Engage in Saudi entrepreneur and e-commerce communities (LinkedIn groups, Twitter/X)

**Follower targets**: 500 → 1,000 → 2,000 by Month 12

### Priority 4 — Arabic SEO (Compounds in Month 4–12)

**Priority keyword targets:**

| Keyword (Arabic) | Meaning | Monthly volume est. |
|-----------------|---------|-------------------|
| إعداد متجر سلة | Salla store setup | High |
| ربط مدى بالمتجر الإلكتروني | Connect Mada to online store | Medium |
| أتمتة المتجر الإلكتروني n8n | n8n store automation | Low-Medium |
| إعداد ضريبة القيمة المضافة للمتجر | VAT setup for store | Medium |
| خدمة إنشاء متجر إلكتروني السعودية | E-commerce store creation Saudi | High |

**Content to create:**
- 10 Arabic blog posts (1,000–2,000 words each) targeting above keywords
- 5 English blog posts targeting: "n8n WooCommerce automation", "Salla vs Shopify for Saudi merchants", "ZATCA e-invoicing WooCommerce"

**SEO timeline**: Expect first ranking results Month 4–6. Traffic compounds Month 8–12.

### Priority 5 — Paid Ads (Test in Month 3–4)

**Google Ads — Arabic search:**
- Budget: $300–400/month
- Target keywords: Arabic store setup queries, ZATCA setup
- Landing page: neotechnology.solutions/services/store-setup (Arabic version)
- Conversion goal: Scoping call booking

**Meta Ads — Saudi + GCC merchant targeting:**
- Budget: $200–300/month
- Audience: Saudi Arabia, interests = e-commerce, WooCommerce, Shopify, online business
- Ad: Quick-Fix offer ($99, fix your store in 24hrs) → leads into upsell
- Or: GCC Special package — "Mada + STC Pay + Apple Pay setup for your store"

**Test period**: Run for 60 days, measure CAC. If CAC < $500 on a $2,499 project, scale. If CAC > $800, pause and refine.
