---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 Company engages Contractor as an independent contractor to perform the services described in Exhibit A ("Services"), and Contractor accepts this engagement.

1.2 Contractor may provide services to other clients during the term of this Agreement, provided such work does not conflict with Contractor's obligations hereunder or violate Section 6 (Confidentiality).

---

## 2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

2.1 Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Company. Contractor has no authority to bind Company.

2.2 Company shall not withhold or pay any taxes, Social Security, unemployment insurance, or other payroll taxes on Contractor's behalf. Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes, levies, and contributions on amounts paid under this Agreement.

2.3 Contractor is responsible for providing its own tools, equipment, and work environment unless otherwise agreed in Exhibit A.

2.4 Company does not provide Contractor with employee benefits of any kind, including health insurance, retirement plans, paid leave, or stock options.

---

## 3. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES

3.1 Contractor will perform the Services described in Exhibit A with reasonable care, skill, and diligence, and in accordance with professional standards.

3.2 Contractor will deliver all Deliverables described in Exhibit A by the deadlines specified therein.

3.3 Contractor will not subcontract any Services without Company's prior written consent.

3.4 Contractor will comply with all applicable laws in performing the Services.

---

## 4. COMPENSATION

4.1 Company will pay Contractor the fees specified in Exhibit A.

4.2 Payment is due within **[7 / 14] business days** of Contractor's delivery of the applicable Deliverables and Company's written acceptance (or deemed acceptance per Section 3 of the applicable project SOW).

4.3 Payment will be made via [Wise / PayPal / bank transfer] in [USD / SAR].

4.4 Contractor is responsible for invoicing Company accurately. Late invoices submitted more than thirty (30) days after Deliverable acceptance are not guaranteed payment.

4.5 Company has no obligation to pay for work performed outside the scope in Exhibit A unless pre-approved in writing.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY — WORK FOR HIRE AND ASSIGNMENT

5.1 **Work for Hire.** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, all Deliverables created by Contractor specifically for a Company client project are "works made for hire" as defined under the US Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 101).

5.2 **Assignment.** To the extent any Deliverables do not qualify as works made for hire, Contractor irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Deliverables, including all copyrights, patents, trade secrets, and other Intellectual Property Rights, worldwide and in perpetuity, without additional consideration.

5.3 **Moral Rights.** Contractor waives all moral rights in the Deliverables to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.

5.4 **Background IP.** Contractor retains ownership of pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and methodologies ("Background IP"). Contractor grants Company a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to use Background IP embedded in the Deliverables solely to the extent necessary to use and maintain the Deliverables.

5.5 **Assistance.** Contractor will execute any additional documents and take any further actions reasonably requested by Company to evidence, perfect, or enforce the rights assigned herein.

---

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Contractor may receive access to Company's Confidential Information and the Confidential Information of Company's clients ("Client Confidential Information"), including source code, credentials, business data, and proprietary processes.

6.2 Contractor will: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it only as necessary to perform the Services; (c) not disclose it to any third party; and (d) promptly notify Company of any suspected breach.

6.3 Upon completion of Services or upon request, Contractor will return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing.

6.4 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for three (3) years, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

6.5 **Client Credentials.** Contractor will handle any client system credentials received through Company with the same or greater care as Company's own credentials. Contractor will not access client systems beyond the scope of assigned Services.

---

## 7. NON-SOLICITATION

7.1 During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** thereafter, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, approach, or accept work from any Company client that Contractor first learned of through the Company relationship.

7.2 This Section does not restrict Contractor from accepting work from clients who independently approach Contractor without solicitation.

---

## 8. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Contractor represents and warrants that:

(a) Contractor has full authority to enter into this Agreement and perform the Services;  
(b) Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner;  
(c) Deliverables will be original and will not infringe any third party's Intellectual Property Rights;  
(d) Deliverables will not contain any open-source software with licences incompatible with Company's commercial use unless disclosed and approved in advance; and  
(e) Contractor will not use any AI-generated content in Deliverables without Company's prior written consent, or will disclose AI use transparently.

---

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

Contractor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company and its clients from any claims, losses, or damages (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from: (a) Contractor's breach of this Agreement; (b) Contractor's negligence or wilful misconduct; or (c) any claim that Deliverables infringe a third party's Intellectual Property Rights.

---

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Services in Exhibit A are complete, unless terminated earlier.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement on fourteen (14) days' written notice.

10.3 Company may terminate immediately if Contractor: (a) materially breaches this Agreement; (b) engages in fraud or dishonesty; or (c) violates confidentiality obligations.

10.4 Upon termination: (a) Company will pay for all accepted Deliverables; (b) Contractor will deliver all work-in-progress to Company; and (c) IP assignment obligations survive.

---

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

CONTRACTOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY TO COMPANY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID TO CONTRACTOR IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.
