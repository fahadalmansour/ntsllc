## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Agreement"** means this Master Services Agreement together with all Statements of Work and any amendments executed by both parties.

1.2 **"Deliverables"** means all software, code, themes, automation flows, configurations, documentation, and other work product produced by Service Provider specifically for Client under a Statement of Work.

1.3 **"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, database rights, and all other intellectual property rights whether registered or unregistered.

1.4 **"Services"** means the professional technology services described in each Statement of Work.

1.5 **"Statement of Work" or "SOW"** means a written document, signed by both parties, describing a specific project scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees.

1.6 **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 **Scope.** Service Provider will perform the Services described in each SOW executed under this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between an SOW and this Agreement, the SOW controls for the subject matter covered by that SOW.

2.2 **Change Orders.** Any request to modify the scope of a SOW must be submitted in writing. Service Provider will provide a revised quote within 48 business hours. No additional work will commence until a change order is signed by both parties.

2.3 **Sub-contractors.** Service Provider may engage sub-contractors to perform portions of the Services. Service Provider remains fully responsible for all sub-contractor work and ensures sub-contractors are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

2.4 **Cooperation.** Client will provide timely access to systems, accounts, content, and personnel reasonably required for Service Provider to perform the Services. Delays caused by Client's failure to cooperate will extend applicable timelines on a day-for-day basis.

---

## 3. PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 **Deposit.** Client will pay fifty percent (50%) of the total SOW fee as a non-refundable deposit before work commences. Work will not begin until the deposit is received and cleared.

3.2 **Final Payment.** The remaining fifty percent (50%) is due upon Service Provider's delivery of the final Deliverables to Client's staging or production environment.

3.3 **Payment Method.** Payment is accepted via Stripe (credit/debit card), ACH bank transfer, or wire transfer in US Dollars (USD). Service Provider may accept Saudi Riyal (SAR) payments at the prevailing exchange rate on the date of invoice.

3.4 **Late Payment.** Invoices unpaid more than fourteen (14) calendar days after due date accrue interest at 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum rate permitted by Wyoming law, whichever is lower.

3.5 **Suspension for Non-Payment.** Service Provider may suspend Services or withhold Deliverables if Client's account is more than fourteen (14) days past due, without liability to Client.

3.6 **Taxes.** All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client is responsible for all sales, use, VAT, or similar taxes arising from the provision of Services in Client's jurisdiction. Service Provider will charge applicable US taxes where required by law.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 **Assignment.** Upon receipt of full and final payment for a project, Service Provider irrevocably assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to the Deliverables produced specifically for that project, including all Intellectual Property Rights therein.

4.2 **Service Provider Background IP.** Service Provider retains all right, title, and interest in: (a) pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and methodologies used to create the Deliverables; (b) general know-how developed in the course of providing services to clients generally; and (c) automation templates and workflows that are not project-specific. Service Provider grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use any Background IP embedded in the Deliverables to the extent necessary to use and maintain the Deliverables.

4.3 **Open-Source Components.** Deliverables may incorporate open-source software. Service Provider will identify material open-source components on request. Client's use of such components is subject to the applicable open-source licences.

4.4 **Portfolio Rights.** Service Provider may reference Client's name and describe the nature of the Services provided for portfolio and marketing purposes, unless Client objects in writing within thirty (30) days of project completion. Service Provider will not publish screenshots, URLs, or proprietary Client content without separate written consent.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Obligations.** Each party will: (a) protect the other's Confidential Information using at least the same care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care; (b) use the other's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; and (c) not disclose the other's Confidential Information to any third party except as permitted herein.

5.2 **Permitted Disclosure.** A party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

5.3 **Exclusions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known by the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) must be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt notice to the disclosing party to the extent permitted by law).

5.4 **Term.** Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement, except for trade secrets which are protected indefinitely.

5.5 **Client Credentials.** Service Provider may receive Client's system credentials (API keys, admin passwords, FTP access) as part of Services. Service Provider will: (a) store credentials in encrypted form; (b) use credentials solely for performing the Services; (c) return or destroy credentials upon project completion or upon request; and (d) notify Client within 48 hours of any suspected unauthorised access.

8.1 **By Service Provider.** Service Provider will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client from claims by third parties that the Deliverables infringe such third party's Intellectual Property Rights, provided Client: (a) promptly notifies Service Provider in writing; (b) gives Service Provider sole control of defence and settlement; and (c) cooperates with Service Provider's reasonable requests.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section.

9.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on thirty (30) days' written notice, provided no SOW is currently in active delivery phase. During active delivery, termination requires mutual written agreement.

9.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days after written notice; (b) becomes insolvent or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; or (c) commits fraud.

9.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) Client will pay for all Services performed to the date of termination; (b) if Client terminates for convenience before project completion, the deposit is forfeited; (c) if Service Provider terminates for convenience, it will refund a pro-rata portion of any advance payments for incomplete work; (d) each party will return or destroy the other's Confidential Information; and (e) all provisions that by their nature should survive (payment, confidentiality, IP, limitation of liability) will survive.

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

10.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delays caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of government, internet outages, or similar events, provided it notifies the other party promptly and resumes performance as soon as practicable.

10.5 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that Service Provider may assign it in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all its assets.

10.6 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and representations.

10.7 **Amendments.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both parties.

10.8 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it will be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions will continue in full force.

10.9 **Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver of the right to enforce it in the future.

10.10 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by email (with read receipt or confirmed delivery) or registered mail to the addresses specified in the relevant SOW.

10.11 **Counterparts / Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed electronically. Electronic signatures are binding to the same extent as handwritten signatures under the US Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN) and applicable state law.
