## 6. YOUR RIGHTS

### 6.1 Rights under GDPR (EEA / UK residents)

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction**: Request that we limit processing of your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
- **Withdraw consent**: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email [privacy@neotechnology.solutions]. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK).

### 6.2 Rights under CCPA (California, USA residents)

California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell
- Delete personal information we hold about you
- Opt out of the "sale" of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
- Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To submit a CCPA request, email [privacy@neotechnology.solutions] with subject line "CCPA Request."

### 6.3 Rights under Saudi PDPL (Saudi Arabia residents)

In accordance with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), Saudi residents have the right to:
- Know the purposes for which their data is processed
- Access their data
- Request correction or erasure of inaccurate data
- Withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, email [privacy@neotechnology.solutions].
