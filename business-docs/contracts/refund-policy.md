# REFUND POLICY

**NeoTechnology Solutions LLC**  
*Last updated: May 2026*

---

This Refund Policy applies to all services purchased from NeoTechnology Solutions LLC ("NTS"). By purchasing any service, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

NTS operates on a **50% deposit / 50% on delivery** payment model. Our refund policy is designed to be fair to both parties — reflecting the work investment on our side while protecting clients against non-delivery.

---

## 2. PROJECT-BASED SERVICES (Starter, Professional, GCC Special, Enterprise)

### 2.1 Deposit (50%)

The deposit is **non-refundable** once work has commenced.

| Scenario | Refund |
|---------|--------|
| Client cancels within 48 hours of deposit payment AND before any work has started | 100% deposit refunded |
| Client cancels after 48 hours OR after any work has commenced | 0% refund of deposit |
| NTS is unable to commence work within 7 business days of receiving deposit (NTS's fault) | 100% deposit refunded |

### 2.2 Final Payment (50%)

| Scenario | Refund |
|---------|--------|
| Client cancels after final payment and before delivery | Pro-rata refund based on % of work not yet completed |
| Deliverables materially do not conform to SOW specifications | NTS will remedy within 15 business days; if unable, pro-rata refund issued |
| Client refuses to accept Deliverables for reasons unrelated to non-conformance | No refund |
| NTS fails to deliver without cause within 30 days of the SOW deadline | Full refund of final payment for the incomplete scope |

### 2.3 What "Materially Conforms" Means

Deliverables materially conform to the SOW when they:
- Include all items explicitly listed in the SOW Deliverables Checklist
- Function as described on at least one of: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge (latest versions)
- Pass the payment gateway live test
- Are accessible at the agreed URL or staging environment

Minor bugs, cosmetic differences from reference designs, or future third-party platform updates do not constitute material non-conformance.

---

## 3. QUICK-FIX SERVICES ($99–$299)

| Scenario | Refund |
|---------|--------|
| NTS is unable to diagnose the reported issue | 100% refund |
| NTS diagnoses the issue but cannot fix it without additional paid work | 50% refund |
| Issue is fixed but recurs within 48 hours (same root cause) | NTS will remedy at no charge |
| Issue is fixed successfully | No refund |

Quick-Fix fees are non-refundable once diagnosis has commenced and a root cause has been identified.

---

## 4. MANAGED SERVICES (n8n hosting, VPS hosting)

| Scenario | Refund |
|---------|--------|
| Service cancelled before the billing period begins | 100% refund |
| Service cancelled mid-month | No refund for remaining days (monthly billing) |
| Service is down or inaccessible for more than 24 consecutive hours due to NTS's fault | Pro-rata credit for the downtime period applied to next invoice |
| Client requests data export before cancellation | Provided within 5 business days at no charge |

Managed services require 30 days' written notice to cancel.

---

## 5. CHANGE OF SCOPE — NOT A REFUND SCENARIO

If you decide you want different deliverables than those specified in the SOW, this is a **change of scope**, not a refund situation. We will issue a Change Order for any additional work. In-scope work already completed is not refundable.

---

## 6. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

1. Email [billing@neotechnology.solutions] with subject: "Refund Request — [SOW Number]"
2. Describe the reason for the refund request
3. NTS will acknowledge within 2 business days
4. NTS will either: (a) agree to a refund; (b) request a 15-day remedy period; or (c) dispute the request
5. Agreed refunds are processed via original payment method within 7 business days

Chargebacks filed without first contacting NTS will be contested. NTS maintains complete project records and communications for this purpose.

---

## 7. DISPUTES

Refund disputes that cannot be resolved by mutual agreement are subject to the dispute resolution procedure in the Master Services Agreement (binding arbitration, Wyoming).
