## Cash Flow Notes

### Why cash flow is healthier than P&L suggests

- **50% deposit collected upfront**: On a $2,499 Professional project, $1,249.50 arrives before any work begins
- **Freelancer paid on delivery**: No payroll during the project — only after client accepts
- **No inventory, no COGS until delivery**: Unlike product businesses, service COGS is near-zero until the work is done
- **Result**: Positive operating cash flow from Month 2 in the moderate scenario, despite low revenue

### Cash Reserve Target

Keep minimum **3 months of overhead** (~$5,700) in Mercury as buffer. This covers the time between a slow month and the next batch of deposits arriving.
